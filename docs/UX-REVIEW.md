# UX review (self-review, not user research)

No usability sessions were run. These are the author's checks against the screens.

## What works
* Five tabs with a bottom bar on phones and a sidebar from 900 px. Sub-tabs scroll horizontally.
* Home answers "where is my next class" in one card, with a Navigate button.
* Everything optional can be skipped. Guest mode needs no data.
* Trust labels are visible where facts appear, so a user can tell official from reference.
* SOS is in the header on every screen.

## Weak points
* The planner has many controls on a phone (search, school, credits, groups). Needs real user testing.
* Sub-tab strips hide later tabs off-screen on narrow widths; the user may not notice they scroll.
* Schematic map is small on 320 px; the zoom has no pan gesture (zoom centres on the selected building).
* Chinese, Korean and Japanese users see English in many places.
* The self-check and address-forms screens are text-heavy.

## Suggested next steps
1. Test the planner with 5 real international students.
2. Verify office phone numbers and opening hours with the offices, then upgrade their labels.
3. Add pan to the map.
4. Finish ZH/KO/JA with native review.
5. Confirm the week-1 date and add the official academic calendar link.
